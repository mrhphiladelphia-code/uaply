[company legal name] ("UApply", "we") runs UApply, an app that helps people find jobs and apply by swiping. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. In short: we collect what you type into your profile, we send it to an employer only when you swipe right on a listing from an employer that agreed to receive applications, and we do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

2. Where your information is kept

  • In your browser. Your profile, swipes and applications are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser data erases them. UApply does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • On our server. When you apply, a record of the application and your swipes is kept on our server so we can deliver it and show what was sent. [Retention: for example "We delete application records 12 months after they are created, or sooner if you ask."]
  • Hosting and fonts. The app's pages are served by a hosting provider ([for example GitHub Pages or your host]), which sees your IP address and device details in its logs. The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address. We do not control those providers' logs.

3. Who sees your information

  • Employers, only when you apply. When you swipe right on a listing from an employer that agreed to receive applications through UApply, we send them the details in your profile. For other listings we send you to the employer's own site and send them nothing. Once an employer has your details, their privacy policy governs how they use them.
  • Service providers that host the app, send email or store data for us, only to do that work for us and under confidentiality duties.
  • Legal and safety. If required by law, court order or to protect people's safety or our rights.
  • A business transfer. If UApply is sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner under this policy.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

4. Sensitive information

Work authorization and sponsorship answers can reveal citizenship or immigration status, which some state laws treat as sensitive. They are optional. We use them only to include in applications you choose to send, never to score, rank or hide listings. You can clear them in Profile at any time. If you are in a state that gives you a right to limit use of sensitive information, this is already how we use it.

5. Automated scoring

UApply calculates a match percentage from your profile with a fixed formula. It does not make hiring decisions. See AI and matching.

6. Your choices and rights

Whatever state you live in, you can:

  • See and correct what is in your profile at any time in the app.
  • Delete your profile, swipes and applications from your browser by clearing the app's data, and ask us to delete what is on our server.
  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your data and not applying again.

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, and other states with privacy laws may also have the rights to know, access, correct, delete, and receive a portable copy of their data, to opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising (we do none of these), to limit use of sensitive data, and to not be treated worse for using these rights. To use any right, email [privacy email] from the address in your profile. We may ask a few questions to confirm it is you, and we will respond within 45 days (we can extend once by 45 more days and will tell you why). You may use an authorized agent. If we decline a request you may appeal by replying to our answer, and we will respond to the appeal within 60 days; if you are still not satisfied you can contact your state attorney general. California residents can also ask which categories of personal information we shared with third parties for their direct marketing (none).

7. Children

UApply is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used UApply, email us and we will delete the information.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including limiting access and, in the released app, sending data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your own copy of anything important, and do not put information in your profile you are not comfortable sending to an employer. If a breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Links to other sites

Listings link to employers' own sites and to listing sources. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. International use

UApply is meant for use in the United States, and your information is processed in the United States.
