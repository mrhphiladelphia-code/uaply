Title (60 characters max)

UAply: job hunting for everyone, one swipe at a time

Short description

Swipe right to save a job and apply in one tap. For students, interns, career changers and anyone job hunting. Built in Philadelphia.

Story

UAply is for everybody. Students, interns, recent graduates, career changers, people between jobs, people who want something better, and anyone who wants to use it. No school email, no resume polish, no special status needed.

Applying for jobs is slow. Hours of tabs, forms and copy and paste, for an answer that may never come.

UAply turns job hunting into one gesture. Swipe right on a posting you want, and UAply saves it and starts your application. Swipe left and it is gone.

What works today: real job postings pulled from public employer feeds, swipe to save, and application delivery to recruiters. [Add your current user count and one real quote from a tester.]

Why Kickstarter: we are a small team in [Philadelphia] building for every job seeker. Your support pays for hosting, a custom domain, more employer feeds and a mobile version.

Where the money goes: [40%] servers and job feeds, [30%] mobile app, [20%] outreach to Philadelphia campuses, [10%] Kickstarter and payment fees.

Risks and challenges

UAply is a small project. Job postings come from public employer feeds, so coverage depends on what employers publish. Sending applications on a user's behalf must follow each employer's rules and our privacy commitments. If the funding goal is not reached, nothing is charged and nothing is built from this campaign. We will post an update at least every week.
